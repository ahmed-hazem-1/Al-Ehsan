# Wanis OCR Required Features

Wanis OCR should support employer-side healthcare administration by helping organizations digitize hospital invoices, review medical claims, validate payment decisions, and monitor healthcare fund performance before payment.

## 1. Document Intake and Digitization

- Upload scanned hospital invoices, medical claims, and supporting documents.
- Convert scanned or image-based documents into structured digital records.
- Extract key invoice and claim fields automatically using OCR.
- Preserve a link between extracted data and the original document scan.
- Support review of invoices, referrals, reports, IDs, and related attachments.

## 2. Document Classification and Routing

- Automatically identify document types such as invoices, claims, referrals, reports, IDs, and supporting medical files.
- Route each document to the correct operational workflow.
- Group related documents under the correct claim or invoice case.
- Separate documents that need medical review from those that need administrative review.
- Flag incomplete document packages before the claim reaches final decision.

## 3. Employer and Fund Management

- Manage employer profiles for companies, institutions, ministries, factories, and large organizations.
- Configure covered employee groups and dependent groups.
- Define healthcare fund settings for each employer.
- Configure approval rules, coverage limits, exclusions, and required documents.
- Track utilization and fund exposure by employer, department, employee group, provider, and claim type.

## 4. Claim Review Workflow

- Track each claim through medical review, administrative review, and payment recommendation stages.
- Show clear statuses such as pending, in review, escalated, approved, reduced, rejected, and clarification requested.
- Allow reviewers to classify claims as approved, reduced, rejected, or requiring more information.
- Support notes and findings for each claim decision.
- Maintain a clear audit trail of review actions and decisions.

## 5. Medical and Administrative Validation

- Verify medical necessity of billed treatments and services.
- Check whether billed treatment is appropriate based on available medical documentation.
- Validate employee eligibility and coverage rules.
- Check authorization requirements when applicable.
- Confirm administrative completeness before payment recommendation.
- Escalate claims that require additional medical or administrative clarification.

## 6. Invoice Audit and Billing Checks

- Review hospital invoices line by line before settlement.
- Detect duplicate charges and repeated invoice lines.
- Flag pricing discrepancies and unusual charges.
- Identify unsupported services or items lacking proper documentation.
- Compare billed procedures, medications, and services against standardized references.
- Check invoice items against employer fund rules, limits, exclusions, and documentation requirements.

## 7. Provider and Hospital Oversight

- Track invoices and claims by hospital or healthcare provider.
- Monitor provider billing behavior over time.
- Identify cost patterns by provider, service type, department, and claim category.
- Support visibility into high-cost providers and repeated billing issues.
- Help employers understand how providers bill across employee populations.

## 8. Review Workspace

- Display extracted claim data side by side with the original scanned document.
- Allow reviewers to verify, correct, and confirm extracted fields.
- Highlight fields that require attention or manual confirmation.
- Support reviewer comments, decision notes, and clarification requests.
- Make final review decisions easy to understand for finance, HR, and leadership teams.

## 9. Operations Dashboard

- Provide a live dashboard for active document and claim queues.
- Show pending, escalated, approved, reduced, rejected, and clarification-requested claims.
- Surface workload by reviewer, claim type, employer, and provider.
- Help operations teams identify bottlenecks in the review process.
- Provide quick access to claims waiting for final payment recommendation.

## 10. Fund Analytics and Reporting

- Track approved claim values, reduced amounts, rejected amounts, pending amounts, and estimated savings.
- Report healthcare fund utilization over time.
- Show spending trends by employer, provider, department, employee group, and claim type.
- Identify avoidable cost patterns and recurring claim issues.
- Provide executive-level reporting for healthcare fund governance.
- Help leadership understand fund exposure before payment decisions are made.

## 11. Payment Recommendation Output

- Generate a validated payment recommendation before employer funds are released.
- Support decision outcomes: approve, reduce, reject, or request clarification.
- Include medical and administrative reasoning behind each recommendation.
- Provide clear outputs that finance teams can use before settlement.
- Maintain decision history for audit and governance purposes.

## 12. Security and Access Control

- Protect patient, employee, and financial data.
- Use role-based permissions for administrators, medical reviewers, finance users, and managers.
- Restrict access to sensitive claim data based on user role.
- Maintain secure workflows for document review and claim decisions.
- Support accountability through user activity logs and decision tracking.

## Core Outcome

Wanis OCR must help employers and institutions review hospital invoices and medical claims before payment, reduce unnecessary healthcare costs, improve fund governance, and make validated payment decisions with clear medical and administrative evidence.
